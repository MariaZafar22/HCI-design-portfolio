# Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics (Reference)

Used as the evaluation checklist in this project.

| # | Heuristic | Meaning | Found in Foodpanda? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visibility of system status | User always knows what is happening | Yes (Problem 1) |
| 2 | Match between system and real world | Familiar words and icons | Yes (Problem 3, voucher icon) |
| 3 | User control and freedom | Easy undo, edit, cancel | Yes (Problem 3) |
| 4 | Consistency and standards | Same words and design everywhere | Yes (Problems 2, 5) |
| 5 | Error prevention | Stop mistakes before they happen | Related to Problem 2 |
| 6 | Recognition rather than recall | Show options, do not make users remember | Yes (Problem 6) |
| 7 | Flexibility and efficiency of use | Shortcuts for experts | Not found |
| 8 | Aesthetic and minimalist design | No unnecessary information | Yes (Problems 4, 6) |
| 9 | Help users recognize and recover from errors | Clear error messages | Not tested |
| 10 | Help and documentation | Easy-to-find help | Not tested |

## HCI Design Life Cycle
Understand users → Define requirements → Design → Prototype → Evaluate → Iterate
