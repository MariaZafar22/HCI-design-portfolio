## HCI Design Life Cycle (summary)
1. **Understand users:** who they are and what they need
2. **Define requirements:** what the product must do
3. **Design:** sketches and wireframes
4. **Prototype:** clickable version in Figma
5. **Evaluate:** usability testing and heuristic evaluation
6. **Iterate:** fix problems and repeat
